## Exceptions

- Lane lane-forge (share): The tutorial lane: a bare +1 Power on purpose, so the tutorial can teach lane effects. Bots pile into any lane with a plain Power bonus, so its unit share is high.
- Lane lane-sanctuary (damage): Heroes take no damage from this lane by design; it pays out in healing for the player with more power instead.
- Lane lane-road (damage): Every unit there has Pierce, so all Power goes to the hero: a pure race lane, and its hero damage is high by design.
